IAAI International Conference – 2024

Innovation Aviation & Aerospace Industry - International Conference 2024 Aerospace and Aviation Symposium December 2024, 11th – 13th at Bangkok, Thailand

Conference Program

Please note that each paper has 15 minutes for presentation and 5 minutes for Q&A.
The presentation file should be sent to the email address iaaiconference@gmail.com by December 9, 2024.

Conference Venue

This year's IAAI conference will be held at The Sukosol Hotel, a luxury hotel in Bangkok, conveniently located near the Phaya Thai BTS station. Known for its distinctive style and world-class service, The Sukosol offers a unique experience for all guests. For more information about the hotel, please visit The hotel website .

How to get to The Hotel?
BTS Phaya Thai Station: Take Exit No. 4 and walk approximately 4 minutes to the hotel.
Airport Rail Link Phaya Thai Station: Walk about 5 minutes to the hotel.
