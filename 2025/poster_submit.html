IAAI Poster Submission

The poster session of IAAI 2025 is now open for submissions, offering an excellent opportunity to showcase your innovations in aviation and aerospace. The session will be held on the morning of November 28, 2025.
You are invited to submit the required information for consideration.📢 Poster Submission
- If your poster is "accepted", you will receive an email (before 19 October 2025 ) to prepare a full A0-size poster and bring it for presentation during the IAAI Conference.
- Please note that all accepted poster presenters must register for the conference at the Participant rate.
